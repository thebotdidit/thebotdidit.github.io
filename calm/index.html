PROMPT · FOR CLAUDE

Reply to a rude message like a pro

Paste the nasty email or text. Claude shows what they really want and writes 3 calm replies that win.

  • ✓ Claude (free)
  • ⏱ 2 min setup
  • ✓ Tested Nov 23, 2026

// setup

  1. 01Open claude.ai and paste the prompt.
  2. 02Add 2 lines of context, their message, and your angry draft if you wrote one.
  3. 03Pick the reply that fits, change a word or two, send.

// the prompt

calm.txt
Someone sent me the message below and I'm angry. Help me reply like the calmest, smartest person in the room.

First, 3 short bullets:
- what they actually want,
- what they're implying or threatening,
- which part of their complaint is fair (be honest with me) and which isn't.

Then write 3 replies:
A) FIRM & PROFESSIONAL – holds my position, sets a boundary, no apology for things that aren't my fault.
B) WARM DE-ESCALATION – acknowledges their frustration, moves fast to a concrete solution.
C) SHORT – 2 sentences max.

Every reply must: keep my facts straight and add no facts I didn't give you (use [brackets] for anything you'd need to check), propose one concrete next step with a date, and avoid sarcasm, passive-aggression, over-apologizing and phrases like "per my last email".

Finally:
- the 2 tempting replies that would backfire, and why,
- if I wrote a draft, what in it would hurt me,
- reply in writing or call? One line.

CONTEXT (who they are to me, what happened):
[CONTEXT]

THEIR MESSAGE:
[PASTE MESSAGE]

MY ANGRY DRAFT (optional):
[PASTE DRAFT]

Change the parts in [brackets] to your own.

Open Claude ↗

// what you get

  • What the other person really wants, in 3 lines
  • 3 ready replies: firm, warm, and 2-sentence short
  • What not to say, and what in your draft would backfire

// pro tip

Wait 10 minutes after reading the replies, then send. Ask Claude to "make version A 20% warmer" if it feels cold.
